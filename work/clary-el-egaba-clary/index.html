ClientClary
SectorHair Care · Egypt
RoleStrategist / Creative
The Brief

Clary, a hair care brand, operates in Egypt's crowded category, making it challenging to attract a new audience.

The Ask

Grab the attention of a new audience in this crowded market while keeping campaign cost reasonable.

The Problem

Amidst a myriad of hair care options, decision-making becomes a puzzle for young women in Egypt.

The Insight

Girls usually ask their friends for advice before deciding things — and luckily, their friends may also be our loyal customers.

The Opportunity

TikTok and Instagram are full of organic unpaid testimonials for Clary's products.

The Strategy

Position Clary as the definitive answer to hair care challenges by engaging with and showcasing the brand's advocates.

The Idea
الاجابة كلاري

Sounds cliché? Here's the twist: we're not claiming to be the solution. Our customers are making that claim.

The Campaign

Step 1: emotional vertical appreciation film for customers (vertical because the testimonials live on IG/TikTok). Step 2: Brand Ambassador digital activation — UGC videos with #الاجابة_كلاري; three random participants become Clary ambassadors for a year. Step 3: Instagram carousels combining a static image + a UGC video in a single post.

How We Got Here

Social listening played a crucial role — it presented the opportunity and paved the way for a cost-effective campaign.
