AlReem Clinics: هنا تبدأ قصتكم

هنا تبدأ قصتكم
ClientAlReem Clinics
SectorHealthcare · Oman
RoleStrategist / Creative
The Brief

AlReem Clinics is the first IVF Center in Oman; started as a gynecological and infertility clinic, since expanded to a wider range of services.

The Ask

A new government-affiliated competitor offers almost all gynecological services at very low prices. AlReem asked us to rescue her from getting lost in intense competition. Beyond the allure of low prices, the true power lies in forging emotional connections.

The Problem

Couples' journey towards parenthood is filled with uncertainty and longing.

The Opportunity

We often view fertility centers as another place for clinical procedures — but what if they are, in fact, the starting point of countless life stories waiting to be written?

The Audience

Couples on the parenthood journey and individuals seeking gynecological care; existing clients as word-of-mouth advocates.

The Strategy

Show that AlReem doesn't just symbolize a chance at parenthood — it becomes a nesting ground for dreams, life plans, and a piece of the individual's legacy.

The Idea
هنا تبدأ قصتكم

(Your Story Starts Here)

The Campaign

Step 1: social posts positioning AlReem as the starting point of stories and the ending point of uncertainty; art direction all positive vibes. Step 2: Instagram Highlight 'الفصل الأول' (Chapter One) — parents share pics/videos from the beginning of their fertility journey; an evergreen, living collective narrative promoted with sponsored posts. Step 3: experiential on-ground activation — 'A Journey Through Hope' interior concept with LED screens around the center sharing heartfelt stories of couples who traversed the path.

How We Got Here

Competitive analysis + social listening on Instagram, Twitter, and Facebook groups to understand how couples feel during the journey; turned the client brief into a creative brief, consumer journey, communication framework & campaign ecosystem.

The Result

Surpassed the competition with a 50% reduction in conversion costs within the first month.
