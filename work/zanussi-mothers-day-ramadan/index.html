Zanussi: Mother's Day x Ramadan

Mother’s Day x Ramadan.
ClientZanussi
SectorHome Appliances · Ramadan
RoleStrategist
The Brief

The home appliance market in Egypt is cluttered with global and local competitors; the market is functional and competes on quantifiable new technologies.

The Ask

Low-engagement industry where consumers can't differentiate products and largely don't care. Deliver a differentiated message building on our HERITAGE and ITALIAN modern brand.

The Challenge

Zanussi participates in a highly competitive advertising season (Ramadan): high cost-per-result in paid ads and low attention spans.

The Opportunity

Create a space where Zanussi transcends traditional advertising — content people would love to watch even after the skip button appears, inviting families to weave their own narratives of togetherness.

The Strategy

Emphasize the mother's role as the anchor of family bonding and togetherness, and how Zanussi appliances help her in that.

The Campaign

Step 1: a jingle embodying the soul of the concept (imagine how far it goes as a TikTok sound). Step 2: collaboration with Zest to create a digital series — a special Ramadan edition focused on family bonding through cooking; each episode features a mother and a relative cooking together. Engage: influencer episodes + digital activation where viewers submit Ramadan recipes with the campaign hashtag; winners get featured in the series.

Footnote

Zest provides creative engaging cooking content — challenges and culinary competitions.

The Result

Client moved to financial quotation stage but didn't proceed.
