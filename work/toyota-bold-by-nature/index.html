Case 07 / 13 — Automotive · UAE

Toyota: BOLD BY NATURE.

Bold by nature.
ClientToyota Land Cruiser
SectorAutomotive · UAE
RoleStrategist / Creative
The Brief

Toyota Land Cruiser, a car that had always been a benchmark for adventure, fun, and boldness across the world, was starting to lose strength in UAE and become uninteresting to its target, and that caused the audience to consider the price gap between the Land Cruiser & its competitors.

The Ask

Reconnect with the young and recover our place as a bold brand once again.

The Problem

Young people are increasingly showing signs of what market researchers are calling 'The Thrill Deficit.' This demographic, known for its high energy and quest for new experiences, is expressing a growing dissatisfaction with current entertainment and lifestyle options.

The Thinking

Emiratis have a long and close relationship with the desert safari. Safaris offer a unique, bold & fun experience. What if we did the biggest & longest safari ever in the desert of the Emirates with Land Cruisers? Bold — but not enough. What if we were bold enough to go into an adventure that no one knows anything about? Bold, for Toyota, would be to never reveal any details about the adventure. Bold, for young Emiratis, would be to go into the unknown with Toyota.

The Strategy

Rediscover the Toyota Land Cruiser's boldness through the introduction of a fresh and distinctive form of boldness.

The Idea
Bold By Nature.

Launch Land Cruiser's longest safari as a mysterious adventure — challenge the Bold generation and their greatest weapon: their adventurous spirit.

The Campaign

Chapter One — We Do Bold, Do You?!: Mysterious invitations to influencers who own Land Cruisers (loaners for those who don't), followers invited to register via landing page and join meet-ups in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Chapter Two — Buzzing It Up: engagement challenges to guess the mystery, partnerships with bold brands and automotive platforms. Chapter Three — Day of the Operation: meet-up points led by Toyota team leaders, unveiled by Top Gear staff and Vin Diesel as adventure leaders; the longest safari ever in the Emirates desert, documented by drones, GoPros, influencer vlogs and UGC. Channel planning on 3 pillars: Subvert (never tell), Less talk more attitude (poke the generation), Go massive (own the day of operation). Tailor-made content per platform: provocative IG stories and reels, TikTok comedy coverage, text-based mystery tweets, guess-the-mystery teasers on FB & IG, long-form YouTube film, UGC resharing. Search takeover. Moderation team 'owning' every answer with provocation manual.

Why It Works

The slogan 'Bold by Nature' captures the Land Cruiser's DNA and the spirit of those naturally inclined to seek thrills. Mystery provokes the daring nature of youth; the safari — a beloved activity — reignites the Land Cruiser's spirited nature, fosters camaraderie and belonging, making the Land Cruiser more than a vehicle, which also reduces price considerations.
