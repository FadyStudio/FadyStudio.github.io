Eltekia Launching Campaign

اختفاء التكية — a launch told as a whodunit.
ClientEl Tekia
SectorF&B · Animated Launch
The Brief

El Tekia launched with a fully animated film set in a vintage, hand-drawn Cairo — hawanem, afandeya, tarboosh-wearing regulars and a neighbourhood khan straight out of the golden age.

The Idea
اختفاء التكية

The Disappearance of El Tekia. A playful whodunit: El Tekia vanishes, the whole neighbourhood becomes a suspect, and the launch turns into a mystery everyone wants to solve.

The Campaign

The animated world gave the brand its own cast of characters and a visual identity that no live-action competitor could copy — golden-age Egypt, told with a wink.
