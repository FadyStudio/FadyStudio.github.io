Case 12 / 13 — Pharma · Rameda · Egypt

Ramelact: Launching Campaign

A toxic relationship with dairy — solved.
ClientRamelact
SectorPharma · Rameda · Egypt
The Brief

Ramelact by Rameda is a lactase supplement that lets lactose-intolerant Egyptians finally enjoy dairy without paying for it afterwards.

The Insight

For the lactose-intolerant, dairy is the classic toxic ex: irresistible in the moment, guaranteed regret after. "And all of that for what?" — "I give up my comfort and my sleep" — "so it doesn’t upset me."

The Idea
متحطش نفسك في Toxic Relationship

مع اللبن ومنتجاته — don’t put yourself in a toxic relationship with milk and its products. We cast dairy as the toxic partner and let the launch film play out the breakup everyone with lactose intolerance knows by heart — until Ramelact makes the relationship work.

The Campaign

A vertical, social-first launch film that follows one man’s quiet suffering through cravings, sacrifices and pizza-box temptation — ending on the simple fix: يبقى راميلاكت هو حله.
